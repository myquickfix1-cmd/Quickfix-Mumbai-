Why Refrigerators Need More Care in Mumbai

Mumbai's combination of high humidity, sea-facing salty air in coastal areas, and heavy dust means condenser coils and door seals wear out faster here than in drier cities. Most fridge breakdowns we see on service visits trace back to a handful of preventable issues — not sudden failures.

Monthly Care Checklist

Clean the Condenser Coil

Dust and grime insulate the coil, making the compressor work harder and raising your electricity bill. Gently vacuum or brush it monthly.

Check the Door Seal

Close the door on a piece of paper — if it slides out easily, the seal is worn and letting cold air escape.

Don't Overpack Shelves

Blocked internal vents mean uneven cooling — leave space for air to circulate around stored items.

Set the Right Temperature

3-5°C for the fridge compartment, -15 to -18°C for the freezer — colder isn't better, it just wastes power.

Wipe Up Spills Immediately

Especially in monsoon months, moisture inside encourages mould growth around seals and shelves.

Use a Voltage Stabilizer

Frequent voltage fluctuations in some Mumbai areas can damage the compressor over time — a stabilizer is cheap insurance.

Suggested Maintenance Schedule

TaskFrequency
Wipe interior, check for spills/odourWeekly
Clean condenser coilMonthly
Check door seal integrityEvery 2-3 months
Defrost freezer (non frost-free models)When ice exceeds ~5mm
Professional gas/cooling checkYearly, or if cooling drops
Don't ignore this: A compressor that runs constantly without cycling off is a sign something's already wrong — usually gas leakage or a thermostat fault. Waiting often turns a small fix into a bigger repair.

FAQs

Condenser coil kahan hota hai fridge mein?
Zyada tar models mein peeche ya bottom-front mein hota hai — manual check karo exact location ke liye, brand ke hisab se differ karta hai.
Fridge ka temperature kitna rakhna chahiye?
Fridge compartment 3-5°C aur freezer -15 se -18°C — isse zyada thanda karne se sirf bijli bill badhta hai, food better preserve nahi hota.
Maintenance khud kar sakte hain ya technician chahiye?
Coil cleaning aur seal check khud kar sakte ho. Gas level, cooling issue, ya unusual noise ho toh technician se check karwana better hai.
