Quick answer: Whirlpool front loaders most often need attention for the drain pump or filter, the door lock, the inlet valve, leaks from the gasket or hoses, and drum bearings. Share the code shown on the display when you book.

How Whirlpool Front Load Washers Fail

The machine fills through an inlet valve, washes with the drum turning on its motor, drains through a pump behind a filter, and locks the door electronically before spinning. Filter blockages and door-lock faults are the most common calls; bearing and seal wear shows up as noise and leaks.

Common Whirlpool Front Load Washing Machine Problems We Fix

Not Draining

Blocked pump filter or hose, weak drain pump.

Door Lock Problem

Latch or lock assembly not engaging.

Water Not Filling

Tap, inlet filter or inlet valve.

Water Leakage

Door gasket, hose or drawer overflow.

Grinding Noise

Worn drum bearings.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

  • Clean the drain pump filter
  • Close the door firmly and retry
  • Check the tap and inlet filter
  • Avoid overloading the drum
