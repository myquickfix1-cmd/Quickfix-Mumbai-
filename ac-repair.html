Frequently Asked Questions

AC Repair FAQs in Mumbai

Why is my AC not cooling properly?
The most common reasons are low refrigerant gas, dirty filters blocking airflow, a faulty compressor, or condenser coils covered in dust. Our technician checks gas pressure and airflow first before recommending repair.
How often should AC gas be refilled?
A properly sealed AC should not need frequent gas refills. If you're refilling every few months, there's likely a leak that needs to be located and sealed, not just topped up repeatedly.
How much does AC repair cost in Mumbai?
General service starts around ₹500-800. Gas refill ranges ₹1500-3000 depending on AC tonnage. PCB board repair or compressor work can range ₹2000-5000. We always quote before starting work.
Do you repair both split AC and window AC?
Yes, we repair split AC, window AC, cassette AC and tower AC units of all major brands across Mumbai.
