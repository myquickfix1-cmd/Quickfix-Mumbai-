Quick answer: IFB dishwashers most often have a clogged filter or spray arm (poor cleaning), a blocked drain hose or pump, a weak heater or empty rinse aid (poor drying), or a worn door seal (leaks).

How IFB Dishwashers Work and Fail

An inlet valve fills the tub, a circulation pump drives water through spray arms, a heater warms it, and a drain pump clears it through a filter. Scale on the heater and clogged nozzles cause most cleaning and drying complaints.

Common IFB Dishwasher Problems We Fix

Dishes Not Clean

Clogged filter, blocked spray-arm nozzles or low water inlet.

Not Draining

Blocked filter, kinked hose or failed drain pump.

Not Drying

Weak heater, no rinse aid or sensor fault.

Leakage

Door seal, loose hose or overfilling.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

  • Rinse the bottom filter
  • Check spray arms spin and nozzles are open
  • Refill rinse aid and salt
  • Check the drain hose for kinks
