Quick answer: Samsung microwaves (solo, grill and convection) that run but do not heat usually have a failed high-voltage diode or capacitor, a weak magnetron or a door-switch fault. Sparking inside often points to food residue or a damaged cavity or waveguide cover.

How Samsung Microwaves Fail

The magnetron makes the microwaves, fed by a high-voltage transformer, capacitor and diode. A door-switch chain makes sure power only flows when the door is shut. Heat failures sit in the high-voltage chain; sparking and turntable issues sit in the cavity and drive parts.

Common Samsung Microwave Problems We Fix

Runs but Not Heating

High-voltage diode, capacitor or magnetron fault.

Sparking Inside

Food residue, damaged waveguide cover or cavity coating.

Turntable Not Rotating

Worn motor, coupler or roller ring.

No Power / Display Dead

Fuse, door switch or control board fault.

Grill or Convection Not Working

Heating element or relay failure.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

  • Check the plug and socket first
  • Clean inside, especially the waveguide cover
  • Never run it empty
  • Make sure the door closes fully
