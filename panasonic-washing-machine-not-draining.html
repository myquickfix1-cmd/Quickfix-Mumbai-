Quick answer: Water sitting in the drum after a cycle almost always means the drain path is blocked — filter, pump, or hose — not that the drain pump itself has failed outright. A full pump replacement is usually the last thing to check, not the first.
⚠️ Safety first: Agar paani leak ho raha hai toh main switch off kar de — taaki short-circuit ya PCB board jalne se bache. Aur agar motor se dhuan/smell aaye toh machine bilkul use na kare.

Why Water Doesn't Drain Out

Panasonic machines (both top-load and front-load) push wash water out through a filter, then a drain pump, then a hose to the outlet. A restriction anywhere along that path — most commonly a coin, button, or lint clog at the filter or pump — stops draining completely, even though the pump motor itself is perfectly healthy.

Common Causes of No Drainage

Blocked Drain Filter

Coins, buttons, hair, and lint collect here over time — the single most common cause of drainage failure.

Kinked or Blocked Drain Hose

A bent hose behind the machine, or a clog further down the line, restricts water flow out.

Drain Pump Impeller Jam

A small object lodged in the pump impeller stops it from pushing water through, even if the motor still runs.

Drain Pump Motor Failure

Less common — the pump motor itself burns out and needs replacement.

Faulty Door Lock Sensor

Some models won't complete the drain-and-spin sequence unless the door lock signal is confirmed correctly.

Control Board Fault

Rare, but the board can fail to send the drain command even when the pump itself is fine.

What a Proper Diagnosis Looks Like

  • Check and clear the drain filter first — often resolves the issue immediately
  • Inspect the drain hose for kinks or external blockage
  • Check the pump impeller for a jammed object once filter and hose are ruled out
  • Test the pump motor directly only after the above are cleared
  • Check door lock sensor signal if drainage still fails with a clear path
Avoid this mistake: Replacing the drain pump motor first — without checking the filter and hose — is one of the most common unnecessary repairs. Filter cleaning resolves the majority of these cases at zero part cost.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

Most Panasonic front-load models have a drain filter access panel at the bottom-front, and top-load models usually have the filter inside the drum near the agitator base — with the machine unplugged and a shallow tray ready to catch residual water, open it and check for trapped objects or lint. This alone resolves a large share of cases.

Is This Covered Under Warranty?

If your machine is still under company warranty, a manufacturer claim usually covers pump or board faults. Once warranty has expired, an independent repair with genuine parts and our own 3-month service warranty is typically faster than the manufacturer's service queue.

FAQs

Filter kahan hota hai Panasonic machine mein?
Front-load models mein zyada tar bottom-front corner mein ek small door hota hai. Top-load models mein filter drum ke andar, agitator ke base ke paas hota hai.
Filter khud clean kar sakte hain?
Haan, machine unplug karke, neeche tray rakh ke filter nikalo aur saaf karo — residual water niklega, isliye tray zaroor rakho.
Pump replace karna padega kya har baar?
Nahi, zyada tar cases filter ya hose blockage ki wajah se hote hain — pump replacement rare cases mein hi zaroori hota hai.
Ye issue kitni der mein fix ho jata hai visit mein?
Filter cleaning ho toh 20-30 minute mein ho jata hai. Pump replace karna pade toh part availability pe depend karta hai.
