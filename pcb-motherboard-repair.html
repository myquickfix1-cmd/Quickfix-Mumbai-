Frequently Asked Questions

PCB & Motherboard Repair FAQs in Mumbai

Can a burnt PCB board actually be repaired or does it need replacement?
Most burnt boards can be repaired at component level by replacing the exact damaged part — capacitor, relay, IC, or fuse — instead of replacing the entire board. Full replacement is only needed if the board is severely damaged.
How much does PCB or motherboard repair cost in Mumbai?
Component-level repairs like relay or capacitor replacement cost around ₹800-1800. Chip-level or BGA repairs on laptop motherboards range from ₹2500-6000 depending on complexity.
Do you repair PCBs for all appliance brands?
Yes, we repair circuit boards and motherboards for refrigerators, washing machines, ACs, microwaves, dishwashers, laptops, and desktops across all major brands.
Why does my appliance keep showing the same error code?
A repeating error code usually points to a specific sensor or relay fault on the control board. Our technician uses chip-level diagnostics to find the exact faulty component rather than guessing.
Is chip-level motherboard repair same day in Mumbai?
Simple component-level repairs are usually completed the same day. Complex BGA or multilayer board repairs may need 1-2 days depending on part availability.
