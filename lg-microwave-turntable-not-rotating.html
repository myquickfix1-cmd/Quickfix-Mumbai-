Quick answer: A stuck turntable with heating still working is usually mechanical, not electrical — most often the roller ring being misaligned or dirty, or the drive coupler underneath the glass tray being worn or broken.

Why the Turntable Stops Rotating

The turntable is driven by a small motor connected through a coupler to a roller ring that the glass tray sits on. Because this is a purely mechanical drive system, a stuck turntable is very rarely related to the microwave's actual heating electronics — heating and rotation are handled by entirely separate systems.

Common Causes

Misaligned or Dirty Roller Ring

Food debris or an improperly seated ring prevents smooth rotation — the most common and easiest cause to fix.

Worn or Broken Drive Coupler

This small plastic part connects the motor to the tray — it can wear out or crack with age.

Turntable Motor Failure

The small motor itself can fail, requiring replacement.

Glass Tray Not Seated Correctly

An improperly placed tray won't engage with the roller ring drive point.

Obstruction Underneath

Spilled food hardened underneath the tray area can physically block rotation.

What a Proper Diagnosis Looks Like

  • Remove the tray and roller ring, clean thoroughly, and check for correct seating
  • Inspect the drive coupler for wear, cracks, or breakage
  • Test the turntable motor directly if the mechanical parts are all intact
  • Check for any hardened food debris blocking the rotation path underneath
Avoid this mistake: Continuing to use the microwave with the turntable not rotating causes uneven heating and cold spots — this can be a food-safety issue with certain foods, not just an inconvenience.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

Remove the glass tray and roller ring, wash both thoroughly to clear any food residue, and confirm the ring is seated flat and correctly oriented before replacing the tray. This resolves the majority of rotation issues without needing a technician visit.

Is This Covered Under Warranty?

If your LG microwave is still under company warranty, a motor or coupler fault is typically covered. Once warranty has expired, an independent repair with genuine parts and our own 3-month service warranty is usually faster.
