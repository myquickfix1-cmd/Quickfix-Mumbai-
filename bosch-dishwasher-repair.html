Quick answer: Bosch dishwashers most often fail through a clogged filter or spray arm (poor cleaning), a blocked drain hose or pump (E24/E25 drain codes), a weak heating element or rinse aid issue (poor drying), or a worn door seal (leakage).

How Bosch Dishwashers Work and Fail

Water enters through an inlet valve, a circulation pump pushes it through spray arms, a heater warms it, and a drain pump removes it through a filter. Hard Mumbai water leaves scale on the heater and clogs spray arm nozzles, so cleaning and drying complaints are the most common. Drain faults usually start at the filter or the hose connection under the sink.

Common Bosch Dishwasher Problems We Fix

Dishes Not Clean

Clogged filter, blocked spray-arm nozzles or low water inlet.

Not Draining

Blocked filter, kinked drain hose or failed drain pump.

Not Drying

Weak heating element, empty rinse aid or a faulty fan or sensor.

Water Leakage

Worn door seal, loose hose or overfilling.

Cycle Not Starting

Door latch, control board or inlet problem.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

  • Remove and rinse the bottom filter
  • Check spray arms spin freely and nozzles are open
  • Refill rinse aid and dishwasher salt
  • Check the drain hose under the sink for kinks
