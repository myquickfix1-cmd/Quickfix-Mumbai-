Cooling & Temperature Sensor Codes (22, 2E, Er 22, E2)

  • LG: Er 22 / Er 15 — Evaporator fan / room temp sensor fault
  • Samsung: 22E / 2E — Refrigeration compartment sensor error
  • Whirlpool: E2 / F2 — Thermistor resistance short circuit
  • Haier / Godrej: E1 / E2 — Internal sensor disconnect

Defrost & Heater Codes (Er dH, 4E, 5E, Rd)

  • LG: Er dH — Defrost heater / thermal fuse failure
  • Samsung: 4E / 5E / 14E — Defrost sensor / drain-line frost
  • Bosch / Siemens: E20 / F1 — Defrost cycle timeout

Refrigerator Repair Questions

Refrigerator not cooling — kya wajah ho sakti hai?
Gas leakage, compressor wear, condenser coil pe dust jamna, ya thermostat fault — sabse common causes hain. Technician on-spot diagnose kar ke exact cause bataega, guesswork nahi.
PCB fault fridge mein fix hota hai?
Haan, control board ke burnt tracks, relay failure, ya sensor signal jaise PCB faults hum accurately diagnose karte hain aur sahi fix karte hain — jahan possible ho part-level fix, warna board replace, dono options cost ke hisaab se bataye jaate hain.
Fridge se paani leak ho raha hai, kya karu?
Zyada tar defrost drain block hota hai ya door gasket damaged hota hai. Technician exact cause diagnose karke sahi part fix/replace karega.
Compressor start hi nahi ho raha, fix ho sakta hai?
Overload protector trip, starting capacitor fail, ya winding fault — teeno alag issues hain, proper test equipment se diagnose kar ke sahi fix milega.
Same-day service milegi Mumbai mein?
Haan, zyada tar cases mein same-day visit ho jata hai. Call karke timing confirm kar lo.
Genuine parts use karte ho?
Haan, sirf company-genuine spares use karte hain — koi local/duplicate part nahi.
Warranty milti hai repair pe?
Haan, 3-month service warranty har repair pe milti hai.
Fridge se ajeeb awaaz aa rahi hai, normal hai?
Nahi, ye fan motor wear, loose compressor mount, ya condenser fan obstruction ho sakta hai. Check karwana zaroori hai warna problem badh sakti hai.
Ice maker kaam nahi kar raha, fix hoga?
Water inlet valve fault ya ice-maker module failure ho sakta hai — same visit mein zyada tar diagnose ho jata hai.
Company warranty khatam ho gayi hai, ab kya karu?
Koi tension nahi — hum out-of-warranty fridges bhi genuine parts aur apni 3-month warranty ke saath repair karte hain, company se sasta aur jaldi.

Mumbai ke climate ka fridge pe kya asar padta hai?
Mumbai ki humidity aur sea-facing areas ki salty air se condenser coils jaldi corrode ho sakti hain, aur hard water se water dispenser/ice maker mein scaling ho sakti hai. Isliye Mumbai mein fridges ko thoda zyada regular maintenance chahiye hota hai — hum yahi dhyan mein rakh ke check karte hain.
Fridge repair ka kharcha kitna aata hai?
Zyada tar repairs ₹600 se ₹3000 ke beech, problem ke hisab se — final price kaam shuru karne se pehle confirm karte hain, koi hidden charge nahi.
Double door aur single door fridge dono repair karte ho?
Haan, single door, double door, side-by-side aur French door — sab types repair karte hain, sab brands.
Fridge continuously chal raha hai, band hi nahi ho raha?
Dirty condenser coils, faulty thermostat, ya refrigerant undercharge ki wajah se ho sakta hai. Isse ignore mat karo — long-term mein compressor damage ho sakta hai, jaldi check karwao.
Fridge mein frost/barf jam ho raha hai zyada?
Defrost timer ya defrost heater fault ho sakta hai, ya door seal se moisture andar aa raha ho sakta hai. Technician exact cause diagnose karega.
Spare parts available rehte hain same-day?
Common parts technician hi saath lekar aata hai. Premium/rare model ka part ho toh thoda order karna pad sakta hai, baaki ready rehta hai.

Depend karta hai fault pe — chhoti problem (PCB, thermostat, gasket) ho toh repair sasta aur sahi option hai. Compressor hi badalna pade toh cost-benefit discuss kar lete hain visit ke time.
