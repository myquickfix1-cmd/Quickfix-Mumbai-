Frequently Asked Questions

Microwave Repair FAQs in Mumbai

Why is my microwave not heating food?
The most common cause is a faulty magnetron, but it could also be a damaged high-voltage diode, capacitor, or transformer. Our technician tests each component before quoting repair cost.
Is it safe to use a microwave that sparks inside?
No — stop using it immediately and call for repair. Sparking usually means a damaged mica plate, metal in the chamber, or a magnetron issue, and continued use can cause damage or be unsafe.
How much does microwave repair cost in Mumbai?
Basic repairs like door switch or turntable motor cost ₹400-800. Magnetron or transformer replacement can range from ₹1200-2800 depending on the brand.
Do you repair convection and grill microwaves too?
Yes, we repair solo, grill, and convection microwave ovens of all major brands across Mumbai.
