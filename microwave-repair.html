Microwave Repair Questions

Microwave garam nahi kar raha, sirf ghoom raha hai — ye normal hai?
Nahi, ye magnetron ka issue hai zyada tar. Plate ghumti hai but heating nahi hoti — magnetron emission drop ho sakta hai. Contact karo, same-day check ho jayega.
Magnetron emission recovery kya hoti hai?
Magnetron ke andar emission gradually kam ho sakti hai bina poora fail hue. Hum pehle chip/component-level diagnose karte hain — agar recovery possible hai toh poora magnetron replace karne se pehle wahi try karte hain, cost kam aata hai.
Microwave mein display hi nahi aa raha?
Fuse blown ho sakta hai, control board fault ya power supply issue ho sakta hai — proper test equipment se check karte hain.
Microwave ke andar sparking ho rahi hai, kya karu?
Turant use band kar do! Waveguide cover damage, koi metal object andar hona, ya mica plate wear ho sakta hai — safety issue hai, jaldi check karwao.
Turntable ghum nahi raha?
Turntable motor fault ya drive coupler wear ho sakta hai — zyada tar quick aur low-cost fix hota hai.
Door properly close nahi ho raha?
Door latch/hook wear ya hinge misalignment ho sakta hai — safety-critical issue hai, jaldi fix karte hain.
Buttons kaam nahi kar rahe?
Membrane keypad fault ya control board signal issue ho sakta hai — diagnose karke sahi fix denge.
Khana asaman garam ho raha hai, ek jagah zyada ek jagah kam?
Waveguide cover damage ya turntable motor ki speed irregular hone se ho sakta hai — check karke correct kar denge.
Microwave se loud humming awaaz aa rahi hai?
Cooling fan wear ya transformer hum ho sakta hai — diagnose karke confirm karte hain koi serious fault toh nahi.
