Quick answer: LG solo, grill and convection microwaves that run without heating usually have a failed high-voltage diode or capacitor, a weak magnetron or a door-switch fault. Turntable and sparking issues are cavity or drive-part problems.

How LG Microwaves Fail

Heat comes from a magnetron powered by a transformer, capacitor and diode, all guarded by door switches. Grill and convection models add heating elements and relays that fail separately from the microwave circuit.

Common LG Microwave Problems We Fix

Runs but Not Heating

High-voltage diode, capacitor or magnetron fault.

Turntable Not Rotating

Motor, coupler or roller ring wear.

Sparking Inside

Residue, waveguide cover or cavity damage.

Grill / Convection Not Heating

Heating element or relay fault.

Display or Keys Not Working

Control panel or board fault.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

  • Check plug and socket
  • Clean the cavity and waveguide cover
  • Do not run it empty
  • Check the door closes fully
