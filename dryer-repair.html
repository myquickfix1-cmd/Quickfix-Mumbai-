Cloth Dryer Repair Questions

Dryer kapde sukha hi nahi raha, problem kya ho sakti hai?
Heating element burn hua hoga ya vent block hoga. Ek visit mein dekh lete hain, zyada tar same-day solve ho jata hai.
Kapde thode gile reh jate hain, poora sukhte nahi?
Vent line block hone se airflow restrict hota hai, ya lint filter clogged hota hai, ya heating element partial fail hota hai — exact cause diagnose karke fix karte hain.
Drum ghum nahi raha?
Drive belt toot sakta hai, motor fault ho sakta hai, ya drum roller worn ho sakta hai — proper equipment se check karte hain.
Jalne ki smell aa rahi hai dryer se, kya karu?
Turant use band karo! Lint build-up heating element ke paas ho sakta hai ya motor overheat ho raha ho sakta hai — ye fire-safety concern hai, turant humein call karo.
Dryer se zyada awaaz aa rahi hai?
Drum roller worn, bearing wear, ya drum ke andar koi foreign object ho sakta hai — check karke resolve karte hain.
Dryer start hi nahi ho raha?
Door switch fault, thermal fuse blown, ya power supply issue ho sakta hai — test karke sahi cause bataenge.
Timer ya control panel kaam nahi kar raha?
Control board ya timer motor fault ho sakta hai — accurate fault diagnosis available hai poora replace karne se pehle.
Dryer bahut garam ho raha hai, normal hai kya?
Nahi, vent blockage se heat build-up ho sakta hai ya thermostat malfunction ho sakta hai — fire risk ho sakta hai, jaldi check karwao.
Door properly latch nahi ho raha?
Door latch worn ho sakta hai ya door switch fault ho sakta hai — zyada tar quick fix hota hai.
