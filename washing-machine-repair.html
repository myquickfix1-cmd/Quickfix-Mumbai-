Frequently Asked Questions

Washing Machine Repair FAQs in Mumbai

Why is my washing machine not spinning?
Common causes are a broken belt, worn motor brushes, a faulty door lock sensor, or an unbalanced load triggering the safety cutoff. Our technician checks the belt and motor first.
How much does washing machine repair cost in Mumbai?
Basic repairs like belt or door lock replacement cost around ₹500-1000. Motor or PCB-related repairs range from ₹1500-4000 depending on the brand and model.
Do you repair both front load and top load machines?
Yes, we repair front load, top load, and semi-automatic washing machines of all major brands including LG, Samsung, Whirlpool, IFB, and Bosch.
Can washing machine be repaired same day?
In most cases yes. Call in the morning and we confirm a same-day visit depending on your location and technician availability.
