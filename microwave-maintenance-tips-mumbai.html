Why Microwave Care Matters More Than People Think

Unlike a fridge or washing machine, a poorly maintained microwave isn't just an efficiency issue — built-up grease and food splatter is one of the most common home fire-risk factors in kitchen appliances. Most of what we see on service visits is preventable with a five-minute weekly habit.

Regular Care Checklist

Clean After Every Spill

Food splatter left to bake on with repeated use becomes a fire risk near the magnetron — wipe it while it's fresh.

Check the Door Seal

A damaged door seal can let microwave energy leak — inspect the rubber gasket periodically for tears or gaps.

Keep Vents Unblocked

Don't place the microwave against a wall or inside a tight cabinet with no airflow — overheating strains the components.

Never Run It Empty

Running a microwave with nothing inside can damage the magnetron — always have food or a microwave-safe cup of water inside.

Only Use Microwave-Safe Containers

Metal, aluminium foil, and some decorative ceramics can cause sparking and damage — stick to labelled microwave-safe dishes.

Clean the Turntable and Roller Ring

Built-up grime here causes uneven heating and unusual noise during rotation — wash weekly.

Suggested Maintenance Schedule

TaskFrequency
Wipe interior and turntableWeekly
Deep clean with steam (bowl of water + lemon, microwaved 3 min)Every 2 weeks
Check door seal conditionEvery 2-3 months
Professional check if sparking, burning smell, or uneven heatingImmediately
Stop using it immediately if: you see sparking, smell burning, or the door doesn't seal/latch properly — these are genuine safety risks, not something to wait out.

FAQs

Microwave khali chalana theek hai kya?
Nahi, bilkul avoid karo — magnetron damage ho sakta hai. Hamesha food ya ek cup pani andar rakh ke hi chalao.
Metal container use kar sakte hain?
Nahi, sparking aur damage ka risk hai. Sirf microwave-safe labelled containers use karo.
Sparking dikhe toh kya karna chahiye?
Turant band karo aur use karna stop karo — ye safety risk hai, technician se check karwao before dobara use karo.
Deep clean kaise karein bina harsh chemicals ke?
Ek bowl pani mein lemon slices daal ke 3 minute microwave karo — steam se grime loosen ho jata hai, phir asaani se wipe ho jata hai.
