Mixer Grinder Dead or Weak

  • Mixer motor weak or slow speed

  • Mixer blades not rotating properly

  • Motor coil burnt in mixer

  • Jar gasket leaking during use

Water Purifier Not Working

RO not producing water, leaking from unit, filter light not resetting, bad taste in water.

  • Water purifier not producing water
  • RO purifier leaking from unit
  • Filter change light not resetting
  • Water purifier making noise
  • Bad taste or smell in purified water
  • RO membrane clogged not filtering
  • Purifier pump not working properly
  • UV light not turning on in purifier
  • Slow water flow from purifier tap

Geyser Not Heating Water

Water heater not heating, taking too long, tripping the switch, or leaking from the tank.

  • Geyser not heating water at all
  • Water heater taking too long to heat
  • Geyser tripping electric switch
  • Geyser leaking water from tank
  • Heating element burnt in geyser
  • Thermostat fault geyser not cutting off
  • Geyser making loud noise while heating
  • Water coming out lukewarm only

Kitchen Chimney Not Suctioning

Chimney motor weak, not pulling smoke or odour, filter clogged with grease, making loud noise.

  • Kitchen chimney not suctioning smoke
  • Chimney motor weak suction power
  • Grease filter clogged in chimney
  • Chimney making loud noise while running
  • Chimney not removing kitchen odour
  • Auto clean function not working chimney
  • Chimney lights not turning on
  • Chimney motor not starting at all

Induction Cooktop Not Working

Induction not heating, display showing error, not detecting cookware, switching off suddenly.

  • Induction cooktop not heating at all
  • Induction display showing error code
  • Induction not detecting pan or cookware
  • Induction switching off suddenly
  • Induction touch panel not responding
  • Induction coil fault not working
  • Induction overheating and shutting down
  • Induction fan not running properly

Gas Stove / Hob Burner Issues

Burner not igniting, weak flame, gas smell, auto ignition not clicking properly.

  • Gas stove burner not igniting
  • Auto ignition not clicking on stove
  • Weak flame on gas burner
  • Gas smell from stove burner
  • Burner flame uneven or yellow
  • Knob not turning properly on stove
  • Gas stove burner clogged with debris

Vacuum Cleaner Not Suctioning

Vacuum cleaner losing suction power, motor not running, dust bag full indicator stuck.

  • Vacuum cleaner not suctioning properly
  • Vacuum motor not running at all
  • Vacuum cleaner losing suction power
  • Dust bag full indicator stuck
  • Vacuum hose blocked with debris
  • Vacuum cleaner making loud noise
  • Brush roll not spinning in vacuum

Iron Box / Steam Iron Not Heating

Iron not heating up, steam function not working, water leaking from steam holes.

  • Iron box not heating up at all
  • Steam iron not producing steam
  • Water leaking from iron steam holes
  • Iron temperature dial not working
  • Iron auto shut off not resetting
  • Steam iron tripping electric switch

  • OTG door not closing properly
