Quick answer: Samsung front load washers show codes such as 4E for water supply, 5E for drainage, UE for an unbalanced load and dC for a door problem. Most faults trace to the inlet valve, the drain pump or filter, the door lock, the motor or the bearings.

How Samsung Front Load Washers Fail

A front loader fills through an inlet valve, washes with the drum turning on a belt-less or belt-driven motor, drains through a pump behind a filter, and locks the door electronically before spinning. A fault at any step stops the cycle and usually shows a code. The drain pump filter and the door lock are the two parts that fail most often in Mumbai homes with hard water and lint.

Common Samsung Front Load Washing Machine Problems We Fix

4E / Water Not Filling

Blocked inlet filter, low water pressure or a faulty inlet valve.

5E / Not Draining

Coin or lint in the drain filter, or a weak drain pump.

UE / Violent Shaking

Unbalanced load, loose shock absorbers or levelling issue.

dC / Door Lock Error

Door not latching, or a faulty door lock assembly.

Loud Grinding Noise

Worn drum bearings or a damaged pulley or motor.

Water Leaking

Worn door gasket, loose hose or detergent drawer overflow.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

  • Clean the drain pump filter at the bottom front
  • Check the inlet hose filter and tap pressure
  • Redistribute heavy clothes and level the machine
  • Wipe the door gasket and close the door firmly

FAQs

Samsung washing machine mein 4E error ka matlab kya hai?
4E water supply ka error hai. Tap, inlet hose aur inlet filter check karo; theek na ho toh inlet valve ki jaanch chahiye.
Paani nahi nikal raha, kya karein?
Pehle drain filter saaf karo. Phir bhi problem ho toh drain pump ya hose block hota hai, jiska technician check chahiye.
Washing machine bahut hil rahi hai, kya normal hai?
Unbalanced load ya shock absorber kharab hone par machine hilti hai. Level aur load theek karne ke baad bhi ho toh repair chahiye.
