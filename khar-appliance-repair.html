Frequently Asked Questions

Khar Appliance Repair FAQs

Konse appliances repair karte ho Khar mein?
7 services — refrigerator, washing machine, microwave, dishwasher, cloth dryer, air dresser, aur AMC plans — ek hi call mein sab!
Genuine parts use karte ho?
Haan, sirf company-genuine spares use karte hain, koi local ya duplicate part nahi.
Warranty milti hai repair pe?
Haan, har repair pe 3-month service warranty milti hai.
WhatsApp pe book karna better hai ya call?
Dono equally fast! WhatsApp pe photo bhej sakte ho, isse technician sahi tools lekar aata hai.
Raat ko bhi book kar sakte hai?
Haan, genuinely 24x7 hain! Raat ko bhi call ya message karo, emergency slot bhi hai jab zaroorat ho.
AMC plan milta hai Khar walo ke liye?
Haan bilkul milta hai — AMC plan appliance-wise customize hota hai, generic ek-jaisa plan sabke liye nahi hota. Call karo, apne appliances batao, hum sahi plan suggest kar denge.
Company warranty khatam ho gayi hai, ab kya karu?
Koi tension nahi — hum out-of-warranty appliances bhi genuine parts aur apni 3-month warranty ke saath repair karte hain, company se sasta aur jaldi.
Spare parts khud arrange karne padenge?
Common parts technician hi lekar aata hai. Premium model ka rare part ho toh thoda order karna padta hai, baaki ready rehta hai.
Bhai mera washing machine spin hi nahi kar raha, fix hoga?
Bilkul fix hoga! Motor capacitor weak hona, drive belt loose ya damaged hona, door lock sensor fault, ya control board (PCB) ka signal na dena — sab possible causes hain. Technician on-spot diagnose karke sahi fix karega.
Fridge cooling nahi kar raha but compressor chal raha hai, PCB fault ho sakta hai?
Haan, ye common hai — compressor sahi chal raha ho phir bhi cooling na ho toh control board (PCB) ka relay ya sensor circuit fault ho sakta hai. Poora board replace karne se pehle exact fault diagnose karte hain.
Microwave garam nahi kar raha, sirf ghoom raha hai — ye normal hai?
Nahi, ye magnetron ka issue hai zyada tar. Plate ghumti hai but heating nahi hoti — humse contact karo, same-day check ho jayega.
Dishwasher mein utensil clean nahi ho rahe, fix ho jayega?
Haan, zyada tar spray arm block hota hai ya detergent issue hota hai. Technician check kar ke same visit mein fix kar dega.
Dryer kapde sukha hi nahi raha, problem kya ho sakti hai?
Heating element burn hua hoga ya vent block hoga. Ek visit mein dekh lete hain, zyada tar same-day solve ho jata hai.
Air dresser steam nahi kar raha, fix hoga?
Bilkul! Water tank sensor ya heating panel ka fault ho sakta hai — technician same visit mein diagnose kar ke sahi karega.
Khar West mein exactly kahan-kahan jaate ho?
16th Road, Khar Danda, Linking Road, S.V. Road, Khar Subway area, Khar Gymkhana, Chuim Village — sab jagah!
Khar aur Bandra West border area mein bhi service milti hai?
Haan bilkul, dono areas ke border wali societies mein bhi regularly jaate hain.
Sea-facing area mein appliance jaldi kharab hone ka risk zyada hota hai kya?
Thoda zyada hota hai — salty humid air se metal parts (hinges, compressor coil) jaldi rust pakadte hain. Isliye hum yahan har visit pe corrosion bhi check karte hain, sirf reported problem nahi.
