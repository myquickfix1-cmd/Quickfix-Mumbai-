Warranty active hai, kya tum repair kar sakte ho?
Agar warranty active hai, company service center use karna hi sahi rahega (free repair milega). Hum out-of-warranty appliances ke liye best option hain.
Genuine parts kahan se late ho?
Trusted spare parts suppliers se — company-genuine quality maintain karte hain, koi local/duplicate part nahi.
Kaunse brands repair karte ho?
Samsung, LG, IFB, Whirlpool, Bosch, Godrej, aur baaki bhi major brands — bas call karo apna model batao.
