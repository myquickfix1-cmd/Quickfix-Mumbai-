Frequently Asked Questions

Wiring Harness Repair FAQs in Mumbai

Is a burning smell from my appliance dangerous?
Yes, a burning smell is a warning sign of overheating wires or a melting connector. Switch off the appliance immediately at the mains and call for an urgent inspection — don't wait.
How much does wiring harness repair cost in Mumbai?
Connector or partial wire repair costs around ₹500-1500. A full wiring harness replacement on larger appliances ranges from ₹1500-3500 depending on the appliance type.
Why does my appliance keep tripping the MCB?
Repeated tripping usually means a short circuit somewhere in the wiring, often where insulation has worn through and a live wire is touching the metal body or earth.
Can rodent damaged wiring be repaired or does it need full rewiring?
It depends on how much of the harness is chewed through. Localised damage can be spliced and resleeved, while extensive rodent damage usually needs a full harness replacement.
Is it safe to keep using an appliance giving mild shocks?
No. Any tingling or shock from the appliance body means an earthing or insulation fault. Stop using it immediately and get it inspected to avoid a serious shock risk.
