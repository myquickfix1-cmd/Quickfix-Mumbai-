Smart Customer Suggestion Guide

Appliance kharab hone par hamare experts ki basic salah:

  • Washing Machine Vibration: Humesha check karein ki machine level floor par ho. Agar sound zyada hai, toh drum bearing badalna pad sakta hai.
  • AC Cooling Issue: Har 3 mahine me filter saaf karein. Agar thandi hawa bilkul band hai, toh PCB ya Gas Leakage ki jaanch turant karwayein.
  • Fridge Non-Stop Running: Baar-baar darwaza na kholein aur piche ke condenser coils ko saaf rakhein taaki electricity bill zyada na aaye.
  • PCB Protection Tips: Mumbai ki high humidity aur power fluctuations se bachne ke liye appliances ko hamesha achhe stabilizer ke saath chalayein.
