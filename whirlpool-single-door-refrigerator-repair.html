Quick answer: Whirlpool single door fridges are mostly direct-cool. Common faults are heavy frost, a weak start relay or overload protector, thermostat problems, door-gasket leaks and low refrigerant; inverter models add a board check.

How Whirlpool Single Door (Direct-Cool) Cooling Works

The freezer box is the evaporator, so cold flows down into the cabinet without a fan. A mechanical thermostat and compressor relay handle most of the work, and frost on the freezer walls directly weakens cooling. We confirm the compressor start first, then gas, then parts.

Common Whirlpool Single Door Fridge Problems We Fix

Heavy Frost

Needs manual defrost; repeating frost points to gasket or thermostat fault.

Compressor Not Starting

Weak relay or overload protector.

Cooling Only in Freezer

Thermostat or choked condenser.

Water at Bottom

Blocked defrost drain.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

  • Set the thermostat mid-to-high and wait a few hours
  • Defrost fully if frost is thick
  • Check the gasket with a paper slip
  • Keep clearance behind the fridge
