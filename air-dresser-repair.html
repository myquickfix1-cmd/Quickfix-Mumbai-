Air Dresser Repair Questions

Air dresser steam nahi kar raha, fix hoga?
Bilkul! Water tank sensor fault, steam generator heating element fail, ya steam nozzle block ho sakta hai — technician same visit mein diagnose kar ke sahi karega.
Kapde poore dry nahi ho rahe cycle ke baad?
Air circulation fan fault, heating element issue, ya vent blockage ho sakta hai — proper equipment se check karte hain.
Door properly close/seal nahi ho raha?
Door hinge misalign ho sakta hai ya seal worn ho sakta hai — isse steam aur drying dono performance affect hoti hai, jaldi fix karte hain.
Control panel touch respond nahi kar raha?
Touch panel fault ya control board signal issue ho sakta hai — accurate fault diagnosis available hai poora replace karne se pehle.
Water tank bhara hone ke baad bhi "empty" dikha raha hai?
Sensor reading galat ho sakti hai — sensor clean ya replace karna pad sakta hai, depend karta hai exact fault pe.
Cycle ke time ajeeb awaaz aa rahi hai?
Fan motor wear ya hanger mechanism fault ho sakta hai — check karke resolve karte hain.
Badbu poori tarah remove nahi ho rahi kapdon se?
Deodorizing filter saturated ho sakta hai ya steam generator underperform kar raha ho sakta hai — diagnose karke correct karte hain.
Machine power on hi nahi ho rahi?
Power supply fault ya main control board issue ho sakta hai — proper equipment se test karte hain.
Cycle beech mein hi ruk jata hai?
Door sensor fault, overheat protection trip, ya timer/control fault ho sakta hai — exact cause diagnose karke fix karte hain.
