Frequently Asked Questions

Dishwasher Repair FAQs in Mumbai

Why is my dishwasher not cleaning dishes properly?
Usually caused by clogged spray arm holes, a blocked filter, weak detergent, or low water pressure. Our technician checks the spray arms and filter first during the visit.
How much does dishwasher repair cost in Mumbai?
Basic repairs like spray arm cleaning or door latch replacement cost around ₹600-1200. Pump motor or PCB-related repairs range from ₹1500-3500 depending on brand and model.
Do you repair built-in and freestanding dishwashers?
Yes, we repair built-in, freestanding, and countertop dishwashers of all major brands including Bosch, IFB, Siemens, LG, and Faber.
Can dishwasher repair be done same day?
In most cases yes. Call in the morning and we confirm a same-day visit depending on your location and technician availability across Mumbai.
Why does my dishwasher leak water from the bottom?
Bottom leaks usually come from a damaged door gasket, a worn tub seal, or a loose hose connection. We inspect all three points to find the exact source.
Why does my dishwasher smell bad even after washing?
This is typically food debris trapped in the drain filter or spray arm holes, or standing water from a drainage issue. A deep clean and filter check usually resolves it.
