Common Problems We Fix

Dishwasher Issues — Diagnosed & Repaired

Every fault has a real technical cause — our technicians diagnose before they repair

Not Washing Properly

Blocked spray arm nozzles, worn wash pump impeller, or low water pressure into the unit — each diagnosed separately.

Not Draining

Drain hose blockage, clogged filter basket, or drain pump/solenoid fault — checked before any part is replaced.

Water Leakage

Damaged door gasket, cracked spray arm, or loose hose connection — precise cause identified first.

Door Latch Broken

Worn latch mechanism or door switch failure — repaired or replaced on-site.

Not Starting At All

Power supply fault, door not sensing closed, or control board issue — tested with proper equipment.

Dishes Coming Out Cloudy

Hard water mineral build-up, wrong detergent, or spray arm partial blockage — diagnosed and resolved.

Unusual Noise During Cycle

Wash pump bearing wear, foreign object in spray arm, or motor mount looseness — checked and fixed.

Bad Odour From Machine

Food debris trapped in filter, or drain hose build-up — cleaned and resolved same visit.

Control Panel Not Responding

Membrane panel fault or control board signal issue — chip-level diagnosis available before full replacement.

Dishwasher Repair Questions

Dishwasher mein utensil clean nahi ho rahe, kya wajah hai?
Spray arm nozzles block ho sakte hain, wash pump impeller worn ho sakta hai, ya water pressure kam ho sakta hai. Technician exact cause diagnose karke fix karega.
Machine paani drain hi nahi kar rahi?
Drain hose block, filter basket clogged, ya drain pump/solenoid fault ho sakta hai. Part replace karne se pehle exact cause check karte hain.
Dishwasher se paani leak ho raha hai?
Door gasket damaged, spray arm crack, ya hose connection loose ho sakta hai — precise cause pehle identify karte hain.
Door latch toot gaya hai, fix hoga?
Haan, latch mechanism ya door switch replace kar denge same visit mein.
Machine start hi nahi ho rahi?
Power supply fault, door properly close na hona (sensor issue), ya control board mein problem ho sakti hai — proper test equipment se diagnose karte hain.
Dishes cloudy/dhundhle nikal rahe hain wash ke baad?
Hard water mineral build-up, galat detergent, ya spray arm partial block hone se ho sakta hai. Check karke sahi solution denge.
Cycle ke time ajeeb awaaz aa rahi hai?
Wash pump bearing wear, spray arm mein koi foreign object phasna, ya motor mount loose hona — sab check karte hain.
Machine se badbu aa rahi hai?
Filter mein food debris jama ho sakta hai ya drain hose mein build-up ho sakta hai — same visit mein clean kar denge.
Control panel respond nahi kar raha?
Membrane panel fault ya control board signal issue ho sakta hai — chip-level diagnosis available hai poora board replace karne se pehle.
