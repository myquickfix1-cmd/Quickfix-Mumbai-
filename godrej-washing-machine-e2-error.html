What Does Godrej E2 Error Mean?

Drain error: paani nahi nikal raha. Godrej mein E1 paani andar aane ka aur E2 bahar nikalne ka error hai.

Common Causes of Godrej E2 Error

Drain filter block

Lint ya coin filter mein phans jata hai.

Drain hose block ya mudi hui

Paani ka rasta band.

Drain pump kharab

Pump paani nahi nikalta.

Top-load mein filter nahi mila

Kuch models mein accessible filter nahi hota, technician chahiye.

What You Can Try First

  • Plug nikal kar drain/pump filter ka dhakkan kholo (neeche tray rakho)
  • Drain hose mod ya block to nahi
  • Paani nikalne ke baad cycle dobara chalao
