Quick answer: The LG Styler uses a separate air-drying system — a fan and heating element distinct from the steam generator — to finish the cycle. Damp clothes at the end almost always mean this drying-air path has a fault, not the steam system.

Why Drying and Steaming Are Separate Systems

It's a common assumption that steam and drying come from the same source in an air dresser — they don't. The Styler's TrueSteam function and its air-circulation drying system are largely independent, which is exactly why steam can work perfectly while drying fails, or vice versa.

Common Causes

Air Circulation Fan Fault

The fan that moves warm air through the cabinet during the drying phase can fail or weaken with age.

Drying Heating Element Fault

A separate heating element warms the circulating air for drying — distinct from the steam generator's element.

Blocked Vent or Filter

Lint and dust trapped in the internal vent or filter restrict airflow, reducing drying efficiency.

Overloading the Cabinet

Too many garments hung too close together block proper air circulation around each item.

Door Seal Leak

Warm dry air escaping through a worn seal means less effective drying inside the cabinet.

What a Proper Diagnosis Looks Like

  • Check garment loading isn't overcrowded, blocking air circulation
  • Inspect and clean any accessible lint/dust filter or vent
  • Test the circulation fan is running during the drying phase
  • Test the drying heating element's function specifically, separate from the steam generator
  • Check door seal condition if airflow and heating both test fine
Avoid this mistake: Assuming a drying fault means the whole steam system needs repair — since these are separate components, replacing the wrong part based on a steam-related assumption wastes money without fixing the actual issue.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

Reduce the number of garments per cycle and space them further apart, and check for any accessible filter that needs cleaning. If drying is still incomplete after these checks, the fan or heating element likely needs a proper diagnostic visit.

Is This Covered Under Warranty?

If your LG Styler is still under company warranty, a fan or heating element fault is typically covered. Once warranty has expired, an independent repair with genuine parts and our own 3-month service warranty is usually faster than the manufacturer's service queue.
