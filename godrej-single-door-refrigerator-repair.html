Quick answer: Godrej single door fridges are direct-cool. If the light is on but cooling is weak, the usual causes are a start relay or overload protector, thermostat, low gas, frost buildup or a worn gasket; Eon inverter models add a board check.

How Godrej Single Door (Direct-Cool) Cooling Works

Cold air flows from the freezer box into the cabinet with no fan. A mechanical thermostat and relay start the compressor, so a failed relay can leave the light on and the fridge warm even when the compressor is fine.

Common Godrej Single Door Fridge Problems We Fix

Light On, No Cooling

Start relay, overload protector or thermostat fault.

Heavy Frost

Gasket gap or thermostat issue; needs defrost.

Cooling Weak

Low refrigerant or choked condenser.

Water Pooling

Blocked defrost drain.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

  • Check the plug and voltage
  • Defrost if frost is thick
  • Test the gasket with a paper slip
  • Keep space behind the fridge
