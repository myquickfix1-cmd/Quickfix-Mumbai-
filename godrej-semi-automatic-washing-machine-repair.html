Quick answer: Godrej semi automatic machines have separate wash and spin tubs with their own motors. Spin failures usually come from the spin motor capacitor, belt or lid safety switch; wash failures from the wash motor, capacitor or timer.

How Godrej Semi Automatic Washers Fail

A wash motor turns the pulsator, a separate spin motor drives the spin tub, and a lid safety switch stops the spin when the lid is open. A timer controls both, and most faults are mechanical and quick to find.

Common Godrej Semi Automatic Washing Machine Problems We Fix

Spin Tub Not Spinning

Spin motor capacitor, belt or lid safety switch.

Wash Motor Humming

Weak capacitor, jammed pulsator or worn motor.

Water Leaking

Drain valve, hose or tub seal.

Timer Problem

Worn timer or loose contacts.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

  • Check the lid closes fully
  • Balance the load
  • Check the drain hose is not kinked
  • Do not overload the wash tub
