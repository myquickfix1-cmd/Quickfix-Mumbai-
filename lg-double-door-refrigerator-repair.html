Quick answer: LG double door fridges are frost-free, many with an inverter or linear compressor. A warm fridge with a cold freezer usually means an iced evaporator, a failed defrost heater or sensor, a weak evaporator fan, or a stuck air damper.

How LG Double Door (Frost-Free) Cooling Works

Cold air is made at the evaporator and pushed into both compartments by a fan, while a defrost heater clears frost on a timer. Sensors report temperatures to the main board, and the board decides compressor speed on inverter models. A failure in the defrost chain or a sensor reading is the most common reason cooling goes uneven.

Common LG Double Door Fridge Problems We Fix

Fridge Warm, Freezer Cold

Iced evaporator, weak fan or stuck air damper cutting airflow.

Ice on Back Panel

Defrost heater, thermostat or sensor not working.

Water Leakage

Blocked defrost drain or tray overflow.

Noise from Freezer

Fan hitting ice or a worn fan motor; compressor vibration on inverter models.

Display or Sensor Errors

Temperature sensor or main board fault on display models.

Poor Cooling Overall

Low gas, choked condenser or compressor and board faults.

Can You Check This Yourself First?

  • Keep vents inside clear of food
  • Avoid overpacking the freezer
  • Check door seal and clean it
  • If ice is heavy, unplug for 3-4 hours and restart

LG double door fridge mein fridge garam kyun hai?

LG fridge ka compressor chal raha hai par cooling nahi, kya karein?
Gas, condenser, sensor ya inverter board ki wajah ho sakti hai. Sahi cause diagnosis ke baad hi pata chalta hai.
LG fridge mein awaaz aana kab serious hai?
Awaaz jo defrost ke baad band ho jaye, aksar ice ki wajah hoti hai. Lagatar tez awaaz fan ya compressor ki jaanch maangti hai.
