Advanced Appliance Repair FAQs in Mumbai

Do you repair smart appliances connected to apps and WiFi?
Yes, our technicians are trained on the networking and IoT side of modern appliances, not just the mechanical parts, so app connectivity and WiFi module faults are covered too.
Can you fix premium imported appliances that local shops refuse?
In most cases yes. We carry advanced diagnostic tools and can often source parts for popular premium and imported brands, though some rare parts may need ordering time.
How much does advanced/smart appliance repair cost?
Smart module or sensor repairs typically cost ₹1200-3000. Complex premium imported appliance repairs can range higher depending on part availability and complexity.
My smart appliance is stuck after a firmware update — can this be fixed?
Usually yes. We can often roll back or reflash the firmware to restore normal function, though this depends on the specific brand and model's update system.
